# Current workflow — reported, not directly observed

## Policy anchor

The 2018 IFHE Hostel Rules and Regulations state that a hosteller who wishes to take leave must inform in writing of reasons and duration, obtain written permission from the Faculty Coordinator/Warden/Assistant Warden before leaving, and parents will be informed. Source and caveat in `../evidence/sources.md`.

## Hostel resident-reported operational sequence

1. Student fills in a leave request through SIS (reported as about two minutes), then prints it.
2. On weekdays, mentor signature is reportedly required.
3. Student visits the warden between about 10:00 and 17:00.
4. Warden reportedly calls the parent; an email may sometimes be accepted.
5. Warden stamps three copies for building security, main gate and student.
6. Information is reportedly entered in two registers.
7. Student may show the copy when returning.

## Reported exceptions

- Warden is busy or at lunch; student waits or returns later.
- Mentor is unavailable; student must arrange another time/contact.
- Parent contact or document requirements may cause delay.

Map handoffs: student → mentor (if required) → authorized leave approver → parent notification → security/gate/student records. Exact roles and sequencing need confirmation.
