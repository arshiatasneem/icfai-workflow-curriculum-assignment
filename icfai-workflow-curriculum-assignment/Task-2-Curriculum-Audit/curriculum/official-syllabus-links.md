## Source limitations

This is a live public webpage. It does not explicitly certify that every current entry is unchanged from the 2023 admission-batch scheme. Its Semester VII/VIII display lists Internship Program / Research and Thesis & Seminar in both semesters and does not clearly settle all elective-credit combinations. Accordingly:

- The page is transcribed as a **published structure**, not silently treated as a verified historical scheme.
- The 166-credit total is supplied for this audit and is used for the target arithmetic; it is not claimed to have been independently reconstructed from the incomplete webpage.
- Missing semester-specific values and batch-specific differences are marked unknown in the map.
- Elective alternatives are shown as alternatives and are not summed together.
- Proposed changes are student recommendations, not existing university policy.

The assignment requires the applicable batch syllabus and regulations. If an authoritative 2023 scheme/regulations document or written programme-office confirmation is obtained, it should supersede this limitation and the map should be reconciled before claiming full batch-level verification.
