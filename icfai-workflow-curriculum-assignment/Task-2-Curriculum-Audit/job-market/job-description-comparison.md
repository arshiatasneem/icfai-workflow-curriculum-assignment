## Three dated-role minimum

The strongest fixed-date evidence in this set is NPCI (20 July 2026), S&P Global (30 September 2026), and Microsoft (updated 22 October 2026). Microsoft is an advanced research position, so it is used only to support longer-term skill signals, not as a typical graduate vacancy. The BMS listings are supplementary because the original posting date is not always available or is displayed relatively.

## Synthesis: curriculum gaps versus absent subjects

- **Present in published structure, needs more practice:** DBMS/SQL, ML, data wrangling, cloud, deep learning and project work. The proposal is to strengthen assessment and evidence rather than falsely claim these subjects are absent.
- **Potential practice gaps:** data quality checks, lineage, repeatable pipelines, experiment tracking, deployment, monitoring, testing and documented limitations.
- **Elective/project topics:** graph AI, federated learning, RAG/LLM evaluation and advanced AI security. These should not displace foundational courses; offer them where prerequisites and faculty capacity permit.
- **Communication:** use reports, dashboards and short presentations to assess whether students can explain findings and trade-offs to non-specialists.

Limitations: postings change, job titles and seniority differ, and this small sample cannot estimate how common each skill is across all employers. The report uses the postings as evidence for specific recommendations, not as a statistical market survey.
