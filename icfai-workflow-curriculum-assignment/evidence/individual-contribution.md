# Individual Contribution

My work included documenting the hostel-leave procedure, referring to official hostel rules, proposing workflow improvements, preparing synthetic test cases, and estimating potential time savings. AI tools helped me understand technical concepts, organise the reports, and improve the clarity of the documentation. I reviewed the generated explanations and took responsibility for the final content and recommendations.
