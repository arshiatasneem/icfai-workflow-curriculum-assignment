# Limitations

## Task 1: College Workflow
- The hostel-leave process is based on my understanding of the existing procedure and the official hostel rules.
- Request volumes, delays, and time savings are estimates, not measured data.
- The five test cases are synthetic examples, and the proposed workflow has not been tested in a live environment.
- Actual results may vary depending on staff availability and implementation.

## Task 2: Curriculum Audit
- The curriculum analysis is based on official IFHE/IcfaiTech sources for the 2023 admission batch.
- Proposed curriculum changes are recommendations, not officially approved changes.
- Job descriptions represent selected roles and may not reflect all industry requirements.
- AI-assisted answers were reviewed but may still need verification against course materials.

The findings should be treated as recommendations based on the available evidence, estimates, and assumptions. Further verification and real-world testing would improve their reliability.
