# ROI calculation — illustrative scenarios only

No measured monthly request volume, delay rate, wait time or support effort is available. These values are assumptions for sensitivity analysis, not observed facts.

Formula: `gross minutes saved = monthly requests × share delayed × avoidable minutes per delayed request`.
`net minutes saved = gross minutes saved − monthly review/support minutes`.

| Scenario | Requests/month (assumed) | Delayed share (assumed) | Avoidable minutes/delayed request (assumed) | Gross minutes | Support/review minutes (assumed) | Net minutes | Net hours |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Low | 50 | 10% | 10 | 50 | 30 | 20 | 0.33 |
| Base | 150 | 25% | 20 | 750 | 60 | 690 | 11.5 |
| High | 300 | 40% | 30 | 3,600 | 120 | 3,480 | 58 |

Example base calculation: 150 × 0.25 × 20 = 750 gross minutes; 750 − 60 = 690 net minutes (11.5 hours) per month. One-time build, training, maintenance, privacy review and integration effort are not included, so this is not a complete financial ROI. If delays are not actually reduced, savings may be zero or negative. Pilot measurement should record baseline and post-pilot processing/wait time, volume, exception rate, staff support and user accessibility.
