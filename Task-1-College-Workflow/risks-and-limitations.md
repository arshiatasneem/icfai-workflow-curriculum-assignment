# Risks and limitations

- **Policy/version risk:** 2018 rules may not be current; confirm official current policy.
- **Authority risk:** tracker must not approve leave or override the named authorized approver.
- **Privacy risk:** leave reasons and parent contact details may be sensitive; minimize, restrict, secure and delete per approved retention rules.
- **Security risk:** unauthorized status changes, overbroad staff access or weak authentication; use role-based permissions and audit logs.
- **Availability risk:** outage must fall back to the approved existing process; a failed or stale state must never imply approval.
- **Duplicate/reconciliation risk:** records may be duplicated or diverge from paper registers; define owner and reconciliation procedure.
- **Adoption risk:** additional data entry could create work rather than save it; measure support burden.
- **Evidence limitation:** operational steps are friend-reported; no direct observation, staff interview or measured volume/timing was available when drafting.
- **ROI limitation:** scenario values are assumptions; one-time implementation costs excluded.
